Understanding Generic Salts

1. What is an Active Salt (API)?

The salt (Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient) is the pure therapeutic chemical molecule that cures symptoms or regulates biology. For example, Dolo and Calpol both use the active salt Paracetamol.

2. What is Bioequivalence?

Bioequivalence means the generic medicine enters the bloodstream at the identical rate and extent as the original commercial brand. They share identical dosage forms (tablet vs capsule) and identical release kinetics (immediate vs extended release).

3. Why are Jan Aushadhi Medicines Cheaper?

Commercial brands add marketing budgets, doctor promotions, expensive packaging, and retailer margins. Jan Aushadhi medicines are procured directly by the Government of India through bulk tenders from WHO-GMP certified manufacturers and passed directly to citizens without branding markups.

warning Narrow Therapeutic Index (Caution Drugs)

Certain medications (anti-epileptics like carbamazepine, lithium, warfarin, immunosuppressants) require precise blood-level monitoring. Never switch caution drugs without consulting your prescribing physician.
